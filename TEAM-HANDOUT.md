# Cross-Training Arena — Team Handout

**Play here:** https://sillius-soddus.github.io/support-cross-training-games/

---

## What this is

Two short, competitive training games for Support cross-skilling. Each game is **10 ticket-style scenarios** with instant coaching tips, streak bonuses, and a leaderboard on your browser.

| Game | Who should play | What it trains |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Classic Circuit** | V3 Support | Classic (**V2**) identification, routing, and reply judgment |
| **V3 Gauntlet** | V2 Support | **V3** — **Legacy** (older UI) and **Evo** (newer UI), labeled on each question |

---

## Product names (use these)

| Name | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Classic** | V2 |
| **Legacy** | Older UI of V3 |
| **Evo** | Newer UI of V3 |

Do **not** call Classic “Legacy,” or treat Legacy and Evo as different products — they are two UIs of V3.

---

## How to play (2 minutes to start)

1. Open the app link from your lead **or** run locally:
   - Open a terminal in the `support-cross-training-games` folder
   - `npm.cmd install`
   - `npm.cmd run dev`
   - Open the URL shown (usually `http://127.0.0.1:5173`)
2. Enter your name (callsign).
3. Pick your game → answer 10 scenarios.
4. Read the tip after each answer — wrong answers still count as training.
5. Check the leaderboard on the home screen (scores stay in **your browser** on that machine).

**Tip:** For a shared machine / team kiosk, use clear callsigns (e.g. `Alex-V3`, `Sam-V2`) so the board stays readable.

---

## Scoring (quick)

- Base points per correct answer, with **streak bonuses**
- Finish screen shows correct count, best streak, time, and board rank
- Aim for accuracy first — speed is secondary
